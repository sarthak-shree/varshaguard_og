About this estimate

What this screen can and cannot tell you

The model uses recent CWC river-level and rainfall readings to estimate whether a gauge may reach its published warning level during the 24 hours after the latest reading.

This is a research estimate. Muzaffarpur has too few historical warning events to measure local accuracy. The model does not predict flooded streets, blocks, or grid cells and is not an official warning. Follow CWC, IMD, and local disaster-management guidance.
