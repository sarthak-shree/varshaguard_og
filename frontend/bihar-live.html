About this estimate

What this screen can and cannot tell you

The percentage estimates whether the selected river gauge may reach its CWC warning line in the next 24 hours. The chart below shows recent readings, not a forecast of future river height.

Rainfall is shown as separate weather context and is not included in the percentage. If readings are old or incomplete, the estimate is hidden.

This is a research estimate, not an official warning or a street, home, block, or grid-cell flood prediction. Muzaffarpur local accuracy is unknown. Follow CWC, IMD, and local disaster-management guidance.
