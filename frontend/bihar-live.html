Prediction semantics: the percentage is the calibrated ML estimate for a documented Bihar flood event starting in the target district within the next 24 hours. Current rainfall observations are processed by the trained rainfall/flood-event model. Current river level, warning/danger threshold status and 1-hour trend are fetched live and are used as independent hydrologic confirmation for the alert state. The supplied historical rainfall and river records do not provide enough overlapping 24h flood labels to honestly train one joint rainfall+river classifier, so the river signal is not falsely presented as a trained feature. Inundation values remain a spatial proxy because no DEM/floodplain/hydraulic simulation is included.
