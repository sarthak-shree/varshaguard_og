About this estimate

What this screen can and cannot tell you

The model looks for a river gauge reaching its published danger level on the next calendar day. It uses recent CWC gauge and rainfall readings plus historical data.

It does not predict inundation depth or flooded grid cells, and it has not been approved for official warnings. For safety, follow CWC, IMD, and local disaster-management guidance.
